## Summary

Vladimir Lenin (1870–1924) was a Russian revolutionary, theorist of [[Marxism]] and the founding head of the Soviet government. He led the [[Bolsheviks]] to power during the [[Russian Revolution]] of <span class="hist-event" data-date="1917-11" title="October Revolution">November 1917</span> and edited the early party newspaper [[Iskra]].

## Biographical Research

Born in Simbirsk in 1870 to Ilya Nikolayevich Ulyanov and Maria Alexandrovna Ulyanova (nee Blank), Lenin trained as a lawyer before turning to revolutionary politics. He married Nadezhda Krupskaya in 1898.

He spent years in exile in Western Europe, where he helped found and edit [[Iskra]], before returning to [[Petrograd]] in 1917.
